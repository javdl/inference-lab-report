      Sources & methodology

        • Single-stream decode and aggregate output are not interchangeable.
        • Eight clients queue behind four active vLLM slots; this does not prove eight simultaneous full-context requests.
        • Repeated-word retrieval and a synthetic receipt do not establish general reasoning or document quality.
        • Prefix caches were not flushed. No confidence intervals or sustained-load results are claimed.
        • Service restart was tested; a host reboot was not performed.
