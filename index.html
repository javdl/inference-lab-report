      Methodology & caveats

      • Decode throughput, end-to-end wall time, and tool-workflow completion time measure different things. They are not interchangeable.
      • Single-stream throughput and multi-stream aggregate throughput are different quantities. Aggregate output is not per-user speed.
      • Context capacity and GPU residency are configuration observations, not proof of long-context retrieval quality.
      • The experiment log records the available evidence, including failures and limitations. It does not supply confidence intervals, sustained-load results, or missing timings.
      • This page is a point-in-time report. The timestamp belongs to the embedded data; reloading displays the latest published snapshot, without polling an inference service.
